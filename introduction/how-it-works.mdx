## Async pipeline

CallPrep uses an **asynchronous pipeline** — when you submit a research request,
we immediately return a `research_id` and run enrichment in the background.
This allows us to call multiple data sources in parallel without blocking your request.

```
POST /research → research_id (instant)
         ↓
   [background pipeline]
         ↓
GET /research-status → completed + data
```

## Enrichment steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apollo People Match">
    We match the email against Apollo.io's database to retrieve the prospect's
    job title, LinkedIn URL, employment history, and company details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PDL Fallback">
    If Apollo doesn't find a match, we fall back to People Data Labs for
    additional coverage — especially useful for smaller companies and non-US contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apollo Org Enrichment">
    If neither Apollo nor PDL return company data, we enrich the company
    directly using the email domain via Apollo's organization API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="LinkedIn Posts">
    We fetch the prospect's and company's recent LinkedIn posts (up to 3 each)
    via RapidAPI — used to generate personalised conversation starters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claude AI Insights">
    We send all collected data to Claude (Anthropic) along with your product context.
    Claude generates summaries, opening talk tracks, synergy points,
    discovery questions, competitive insights, and translates everything
    to your preferred language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision Makers (Growth+)">
    For users on Growth plan and above, we scrape key people at the company
    (Directors, VPs, C-level) via Apify's LinkedIn scraper.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Caching

CallPrep caches enriched data to reduce costs and latency.
If a prospect or company has been researched before (within cache TTL),
results return **immediately** — usually under 2 seconds — and no data source APIs are called.

| Data type        | Cache TTL |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Prospect data   | 30 days   |
| Company data    | 90 days   |
| Decision makers | 60 days   |

Caching is scoped to your account and product — different users never share cached data,
even for the same email address.

## Product personalisation

Each API key is tied to a **product context** — your product's name, description,
key features, and target customers. This context is passed to Claude for every research request,
so all AI-generated content (synergy points, discovery questions, opening talks)
is tailored to **your specific product**, not generic sales advice.

<Note>
  To update your product context, go to [API Keys](https://call-prep-api.vercel.app/api-keys)
  in the dashboard and edit the key's product settings.
</Note>
