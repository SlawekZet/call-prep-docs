## Planned features

- **Pre-call brief** — automatically post a research brief to a Slack channel or DM
  before a scheduled meeting
- **Slash command** — `/callprep john@acme.com` to research a prospect on demand
- **Deal alerts** — get notified when a researched prospect visits your website or opens an email

## Workaround using n8n

You can already post CallPrep briefs to Slack using n8n.
See the [n8n integration guide](/integrations/n8n) for a step-by-step walkthrough.
