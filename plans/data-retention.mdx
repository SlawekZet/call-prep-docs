## Cached data TTLs

| Data type        | Cache TTL | After expiry                        |
|-----------------|-----------|--------------------------------------|
| Prospect data   | 30 days   | Re-fetched on next research request  |
| Company data    | 90 days   | Re-fetched on next research request  |
| Decision makers | 60 days   | Re-fetched on next research request  |

## Research job data

Research jobs and their results are retained for **90 days** after completion.
After that, the result payload is cleared but the job record (ID, status, timestamps) is kept
for billing and audit purposes.

## Research logs

Pipeline execution logs (API call counts, costs per step, duration) are retained for **12 months**.

## Error logs

Error logs are retained for **6 months**.

## GDPR

CallPrep processes personal data (prospect names, email addresses, LinkedIn profiles)
on your behalf as a **data processor**. You are the data controller.

Key points:
- Data is stored in the EU (Supabase EU West region)
- You can delete all data for a specific prospect by contacting [support@callprep.io](mailto:support@callprep.io)
- Account deletion removes all associated data within 30 days (per your request)
- We do not sell or share data with third parties

For a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), contact [support@callprep.io](mailto:support@callprep.io).
